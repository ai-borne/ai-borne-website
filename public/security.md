# Security Policy

To report a suspected vulnerability, email [founder@ai-borne.in](mailto:founder@ai-borne.in). The published PGP key is available at [/.well-known/pgp-key.txt](/.well-known/pgp-key.txt).

Please provide the affected URL or component, reproducible steps, and a safe proof of concept. Do not include credentials, access tokens, or unnecessary personal data. Avoid public disclosure until the issue can be coordinated.

This policy covers the AI-Borne website and its hosted API endpoints. Reports are assessed for reproducibility and impact; no response-time or remediation-time commitment is implied.
